# Revenue Track

## Product 1 — Conversation Story

A privacy-first relationship/chat analysis product built around a reusable pattern engine and pluggable AI interpretation layer.

### MVP sequence

1. Robust WhatsApp ZIP/TXT import and diagnostics.
2. One-screen-at-a-time participant/context wizard.
3. Relationship-specific deterministic First Read.
4. Saved reports and deletion.
5. Evidence-window and turning-point candidate extraction.
6. Low-cost AI provider integration.
7. Free AI teaser + deeper paid report.
8. Payment/authentication.
9. Shareable result cards, follow-up questions and conversion testing.

### Product principles

- Never hide a partial import.
- Never call deterministic statistics “AI”.
- Pattern before interpretation.
- Evidence before verdict.
- Confidence and alternative explanations stay visible.
- Relationship context changes the analysis rubric.
- Raw chats are not persistent product storage.
- Commercial customer data stays separate from JARVIS private memory and device data.
- Provider abstraction should allow Groq/Mistral/OpenAI/local models without rewriting the product.

## Additional digital assets worth testing later

### Group Chat Wrapped
Shareable group/yearly reports: roles, awards, activity trends, night owls, session starters and inside-joke patterns. Reuses the same parser and timeline engine.

### eBay Listing Doctor
Photos/listing → title, attributes, shipping/fees, pricing and profit report. Later connects to JARVIS Commerce.

### Supplier QA Document Checker
PO + COC/COA + drawing/spec → deterministic mismatch detection. Strong B2B niche and higher possible willingness to pay.

### Difficult Conversation Coach
Paste a draft + context → likely ambiguity, escalation triggers, clearer rewrite and alternative tone options. Can reuse the relationship lenses without needing a full chat export.

### Relationship Timeline / Memory Book
Turn years of chat into a private chronology of milestones, recurring jokes, trips, plans and memorable periods. More emotional/keepsake-oriented than the analytical product.

Priority: finish Conversation Story before spawning multiple half-built products.
