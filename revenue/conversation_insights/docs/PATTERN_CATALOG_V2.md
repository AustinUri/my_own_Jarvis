# Pattern Catalog V2

Patterns should be measured longitudinally and, when AI is enabled, validated against message windows.

## Core patterns
- Initiative asymmetry
- Reply rhythm by context (not raw average only)
- Burst -> silence -> reconnect cycles
- Conversation closure vs revival
- Curiosity / question reciprocity
- Planning initiation and follow-through
- Warmth / affection reciprocity
- Gratitude and appreciation
- Vulnerability and response
- Repair after conflict
- Apology asymmetry
- Pursue / withdraw cycles
- Boundary setting and boundary respect
- Repeated unresolved topics
- Escalation / de-escalation
- Practical support / care work
- Private language and running jokes
- Topic avoidance
- Late-night / after-hours patterns
- Then-vs-now change
- Candidate turning points
- Group centrality: who holds the group together, who disappears, who coordinates

## Relationship-specific examples

### Ex
- Who reopens contact after silence?
- Does warmth return only when the other person pulls away?
- Are boundaries stated and then respected?
- Does the conversation repeatedly return to the breakup without resolution?

### Dating
- Does planning turn into real follow-through?
- Is curiosity reciprocal?
- Does one person become warmer only after distance?
- How do both people behave after friction?

### Work
- Who owns next steps?
- Who follows up after ambiguity?
- Is "urgent" communication actually reciprocal?
- Are boundaries eroding outside working hours?

## Anti-patterns in the analyzer
Never infer:
- attachment style from a few texts,
- a psychiatric diagnosis,
- lying or cheating without evidence,
- "who loves more" from message count,
- motive as fact.

Use language such as "consistent with", "may indicate", "the chat shows", and include alternatives.
