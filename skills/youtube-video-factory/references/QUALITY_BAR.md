# Visual quality bar for preschool animation

Before any video may enter a publication queue:

- **Originality**: no imitation of a recognizably existing show, character look, brand, music or speech style. Asset provenance and commercial licenses recorded.
- **Consistent cast**: stable character identity, proportions, palette and expressions, not random generation every scene.
- **Action readable on mute**: visible cause and effect, a meaningful prop matching each plot beat, and natural staged movement. Not six versions of the same static frame.
- **Camera storytelling**: opening establishing shot, action coverage, close reactions and purposeful changes of framing.
- **2D animation production**: approved rigged characters, mouth and eye states, articulated limbs, squash/stretch, locomotion paths, anticipation and follow-through. Placeholder circles and bouncing clip-art are never publication assets.
- **Sound**: child-friendly expressive English narration, appropriately licensed sound effects and music, sensible loudness, no clipped phonemes, speech synchronized to beats.
- **Technical**: test on phone, safe-zone text, clean captions, no cropping, no awkward aspect ratios, real 1080×1920 master if making Shorts, smooth 24/30fps output as budgets allow.
- **Content**: age-appropriate and respectful; no frightening, unsafe, deceptive or commercial prompts. Human reviewers decide child-directed setting and quality.
- **High-quality repeatability**: demonstrate three significantly different completed episodes, not three color variants, before considering scale.

The storybook rig produces local review drafts. Inspect the encoded frames where a hand grasps an object, each change of expression and each scene transition. Check the soundtrack at each cut. The technical report samples one instant per scene for prop visibility and four for motion. A reviewer evaluates the rest of the performance and the story as a whole.
